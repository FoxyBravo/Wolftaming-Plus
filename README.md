# WolfTaming Plus

WolfTaming Plus is a community fork of WolfTaming, originally created by G3rste. It builds upon the original mod while refining existing features, improving companion immersion, and introducing quality-of-life improvements that work alongside TamedAI Plus.

A huge thank you to G3rste for creating and maintaining WolfTaming, one of the most iconic pet and companion mods in the Vintage Story community.

## What's New

### Improved Chewing Bone

* The old Dog Toy has been replaced with the **Chewing Bone**.
* Supports the enhanced throwing and fetch mechanics provided by TamedAI Plus.
* The Chewing Bone now has durability and will eventually wear out with repeated use.
* Can be placed on shelves and in display cases for decoration.

### Better Crafting

* The Chewing Bone recipe now uses **Raw Red Meat**, making it feel more natural and fitting for a dog treat and toy.

### Refreshed Animal Textures

* Updated textures for:

  * Corgis
  * Hunting Hounds
  * Shepherds
* Improved visual consistency and overall appearance.

### Streamlined Dog Breeds

* The Pekingese variant has been removed.
* This helps keep the mod focused on the core companion breeds while reducing maintenance complexity.

### Improved Naming & Immersion

Several animals and items have been renamed to better fit the spirit of the mod:

* **Wolfdog** → **Tamed Wolf**
* **Hunting Dog** → **Hunting Hound**
* **German Shepherd** → **Shepherd**
* **Dog Toy** → **Chewing Bone**

### Improved Equipment Interface

New labels have been added to make pet equipment easier to understand:

* Bags
* Body Armor
* Helmet
* Collar

## Disclaimer

* AI was used to assist in the development and improvement of this mod.
* Translations other than English have not been verified.
* This mod is primarily designed to be used alongside TamedAI Plus.
* Compatibility with all third-party pet and animal mods has not yet been fully tested.
